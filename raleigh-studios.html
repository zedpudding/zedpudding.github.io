Raleigh Studios

Entertainment branding, identity design, venue positioning, location systems, and studio-world collateral

A historic production studio brand rebuilt for a larger entertainment footprint.

Role:

Creative Director

Agency689 Inc.

Work:
  • Entertainment brand identity
  • Studio positioning
  • Location brand system
  • Signage and environmental language
  • Marketing collateral
  • Digital presentation assets
  • Brand expansion support

Raleigh Studios needed a complete rebrand for the world’s largest independent movie studio, along with a system that could extend across seven new locations.

The challenge was to respect the working-studio legacy while giving the brand enough structure to operate as a broader entertainment property. The identity had to feel established, useful, and credible in a world where production companies, agencies, crews, and clients all read the details quickly.

The work created a cleaner studio presence and a more scalable brand platform: identity, venue positioning, collateral, location language, and visual rules that could move from lot-facing materials to broader marketing without losing the studio’s practical, production-first character.

Built for the lot and the pitch.

Studio brands need to do quiet work in a lot of places. They appear on presentations, gate signage, collateral, production materials, location information, and sales conversations where the audience already understands the industry.

The Raleigh system kept the brand confident and direct, with enough polish to support expansion and enough restraint to still feel like a working studio.

One studio, seven new locations.

The system had to stretch without getting noisy. The master brand needed enough authority to hold the family together, while individual locations needed room to feel local, functional, and easy to understand for production clients.
