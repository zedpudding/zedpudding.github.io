Agency689 was tapped for strategy work on Xbox’s new Elite gaming program, centered around the launch of the Elite Controller and aimed squarely at hardcore players.

The work looked beyond a single hardware launch. It explored how software updates, controller features, team identity, apparel, and campaign language could work together as a broader Elite platform.

The goal was to help Xbox speak to serious gamers with a system that felt earned: competitive, direct, and connected to the culture around performance, gear, and belonging.

More than a controller launch.

The Elite Controller gave the program a physical center, but the opportunity was bigger than hardware. Xbox needed a way to frame Elite as a mindset and a community for players who care deeply about performance.

The strategy connected product, software, team identity, and apparel into a launch platform that could grow after the first wave of attention.

Built for elite players.

The work had to respect gaming culture without dressing it up from the outside. Everything centered on performance, commitment, and the visual language of a team that serious players would actually want to join.
