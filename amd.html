AMD needed a way to tell the Scorpius chipset story to a Comic-Con audience without sounding like a spec sheet. The answer was a custom comic: super-processing ninjas, a clear hero/villain structure, and a visual world that could make performance feel like entertainment.

Agency689 drew and painted the art, built the story, and managed production for more than half a million units for San Diego Comic-Con, followed by a worldwide Comic-Con rollout of more than two million units.

Built for the floor.

The comic had to work fast: bold enough to catch people in the chaos of Comic-Con, clear enough to explain the AMD story, and collectible enough that people would keep it. The “villain” lived in Intel blue, the heroes carried the AMD world, and the whole thing turned a processor campaign into something people could actually pick up and read.
