Killer Networks

Brand identity, product naming, packaging, web, advertising, event concepts, and campaign systems

Turned a gaming network card into a brand with one simple enemy: lag.

Role:

Creative Director

Agency689 Inc.

Work:
  • Brand identity
  • Product naming
  • Packaging system
  • Website + digital campaign
  • Advertising collateral
  • Event campaign concepts
  • Illustration + animation direction

Bigfoot Networks had a high-performance network interface card built for online gaming. The product story was technical, but the audience needed something faster than specs. They needed to know what it killed.

The work shaped Killer into a gamer-facing brand system around a direct promise: end lag. Naming, packaging, advertising, web, video, event concepts, character art, and campaign language all pushed the product out of component-world and into the emotional territory of competition.

The brand strategy was built for momentum. The original plan was to position Killer for sale in five years. It sold in three, later moving through Qualcomm and continuing as a recognizable gaming networking brand.

The idea was simple: kill lag.

The commercial takes the product benefit and turns it into a gamer problem anyone could feel immediately. Instead of explaining packet handling or network processing, the spot makes lag the enemy and positions Killer as the hardware built to take it out.

It is very much of its era, but that is part of the story: a technical component pushed into a louder entertainment space through voice, character, attitude, and a blunt promise.

Not just a faster card.

The brand needed a target. Lag became the villain, and every piece of the system gave gamers a reason to see networking performance as part of the fight: packaging, campaign art, product names, conference ideas, banner systems, and the kind of swag people actually wanted to keep.
