03 // About
Multi- Multi- Disciplinary Disciplinary Designer

Hello — I'm Zander Vera, identity designer and founder of Agency689, a full-service branding studio based in Southern California. For over 25 years I've helped companies, hotels, restaurants, and creators answer the one question that matters most: who are you?

My work spans naming, logo design, brand strategy, brand books, UI/UX, packaging, and campaigns. I believe identity is the most important thing a brand can own, and I treat it that way.
