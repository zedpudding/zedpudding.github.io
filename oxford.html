Oxford Collection

Brand identity system, brand architecture, primary logos, supporting marks, brand books

A unified hospitality brand system built to hold a growing West Coast portfolio.

Role:

Creative Director

AGENCY689 Inc.

Work:
  • Brand Identity System
  • Primary Logos + Supporting Marks
  • Brand Architecture
  • Typography + Color Standards
  • Comprehensive Brand Books

Oxford Collection operates 16 hotels across the West Coast and needed a clearer identity system to strengthen recognition across the portfolio without flattening the personality of each property.

Four primary logos and supporting marks were developed to establish a clean brand hierarchy, giving the collection, hotel, suites, and future concepts enough structure to work together and enough flexibility to live on their own.

The result is a cohesive, scalable brand foundation built for long-term growth, property-level distinction, and more consistent guest-facing communication.

One family.
Clear rooms.

The work was really about brand architecture: making sure guests, owners, and internal teams could understand which Oxford they were looking at and how each piece connected back to the larger collection.

Built for
consistency.

Comprehensive brand books defined logo usage, typography, colors, supporting marks, and practical rules for applying the identity across property collateral, campaigns, internal teams, and future growth.
