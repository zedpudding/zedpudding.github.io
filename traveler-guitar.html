Traveler Guitar

Brand identity, product logo system, product badging, headstock branding, marketing collateral

Built the master brand and product identity system for a guitar line made to go anywhere.

Role:

Creative Director

Agency689 Inc.

Work:
  • Master brand mark
  • Product logo system
  • Sub-brand development
  • Product badging + headstock branding
  • Anniversary marks
  • Marketing collateral

Traveler Guitar builds instruments for players who want to keep music within reach: on the road, backstage, in an apartment, in a hotel room, or anywhere a full-size guitar becomes too much object for the moment. The brand had strong recognition in the travel-guitar space, but its growing product family needed a clearer system around it.

I worked with the team to turn a logo refresh into a broader identity system. The work included a master brand mark, a complete family of product logos, sub-brand development, product badging, headstock branding, and marketing collateral built to hold together across retail, packaging, ecommerce, photography, and the instruments themselves.

The goal was to make the small guitar feel like the real one. The identity needed to be simple enough to live on hardware and headstocks, but strong enough to carry the brand across a full product ecosystem. The final system created a more consistent visual language while giving each line enough room to keep its own character.

Not just a logo.

Traveler needed a brand that could live at every scale: on a headstock, on a product page, on packaging, in a catalog, and across an expanding line of instruments. The work made room for the master brand while giving each model a clearer personality of its own.
