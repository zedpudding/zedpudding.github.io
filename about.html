—About

Zander Vera has made peace with having too many interests.

Zander Vera is a creative director, brand builder, writer, musician, and partner at Agency689. He works across identity, naming, logos, digital, print, packaging, signage, campaigns, AI-assisted creative systems, and the small, maddening details that decide whether a thing feels finished or just approved.

Now
Agency689, Partner + Creative Director
Built a studio around the idea that identity is more than a logo, though he has also made a great many logos. Names, systems, campaigns, websites, signs, packaging, rooms, products, and whatever else a brand needs to become real. Now he is using AI the same way he used the web when it first got interesting: as a tool for momentum, sharper thinking, faster prototypes, and better creative decisions.
Digital shift
DGWB, Interactive Creative Director
Led brands into the interactive era before "digital" became the department everyone claimed to understand. Clients came to him to make the new stuff useful: websites with purpose, campaigns that moved between media, and brand systems that could survive outside a printed page.
First wave
firstsource.com, Creative Director
Helped turn emerging technology into language people could understand and experiences they could use. Strategy, interface, content, and design all had to work together, which suited him fine. That has pretty much been the job ever since.

What he's made

Along the way, he has worked on brand systems for Traveler Guitar, Sunset Marquis, Killer Networking, AMD, XBOX, Hotel Figueroa, Oxford Collection, and others.

He has won some awards, written a book of poems and sketches, made music, and continued to believe that the best creative people do not defend the old way or chase the new one blindly. They learn the tool, test the edge, and make the work better.
