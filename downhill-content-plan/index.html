4-week social content plan
Built around the actual poems, art, humor, and visual language of the book.

The idea: don’t make the feed feel like a month-long ad. Most posts should work as entertainment, writing, art, humor, or a personal moment on their own. The book plug stays present, but rarely becomes the whole point.

Visual system

  • 1080 × 1920 for Reels / TikTok / Shorts
  • Graphite art stays the hero
  • Off-white / cream background
  • Red as a restrained accent, not “murder scene” splatter
  • Slow push-ins, pans, fades, simple type reveals
  • Cover appears sparingly so it still feels special

Content ratio

  • Most posts: entertainment / writing / art / humor
  • Only a handful: direct “buy the book” posts
  • Rotate funny, bruised, romantic, weird, and sincere
  • Reuse the same art with different crops rather than inventing new visual worlds
