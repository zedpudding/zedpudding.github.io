Clink Hostels

Strategy, web redesign, UX, WordPress build, booking flow, and digital brand experience

Making hostel friendly, social, useful, and easier to book.

Clink started with a wonderfully odd premise: two sisters from Dublin bought a courthouse and jail in London and turned a night in jail into a splendid hostel stay. Years later, the brand had locations in London and Amsterdam, with Dublin and Lisbon on the way.

When Clink needed to discover new guest markets, expand its digital presence, and build a site that stood out in a crowded travel category, Agency689 helped reshape the web experience around the social side of hostel life.

The work focused on guest journeys, emerging traveler segments, experience-led content, easier updates, and a booking path that let the digital brand become a real marketing hub.

Stand out as
the place to be.

To appeal to hostel travelers in a congested market, Clink needed to elevate customer interaction, introduce a more compelling look at the Clink lifestyle, and keep the artistically infused brand in the foreground.

The THINK
behind the
CLINK

We reinvented the user journey to focus on the social side of Clink, something that was a core value at each property but not well reflected on the web. Our research team uncovered untapped potential in women and solo travelers and elevated ideas like the importance of breakfast and hostel experiences in the user journey.

The new Clink UX is focused on their current and aspired audience and engages them with the experiences they associate with exceptional hostel experiences to increase engagement and conversion.

Under the hood.

We created the Clink website on WordPress with a YooTheme backend, giving us complete design control while making updates and site changes easy for the client. The site was built to stay flexible as content, art, offers, locations, and guest-facing stories continue to evolve.

The site is optimized for ADA compliance, connected to YOAST, and tracked with GA4. We also installed media tools, cookie opt-out tools, site forms, AI-based chat, and the booking engine with custom widgets, creating a branded digital hub the client can update without monthly fees outside hosting and plugins.
