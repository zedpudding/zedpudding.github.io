Hotel Figueroa

Brand identity, brand book, collateral, campaign, launch materials

Built a brand blueprint for a downtown Los Angeles landmark ready for its next act.

Role:

Creative Director

AGENCY689 Inc.

Work:
  • Brand identity
  • Brand book
  • Hotel collateral
  • Launch materials
  • Campaigns
  • Guest touchpoints

Hotel Figueroa needed a brand system that could hold the weight of a historic downtown Los Angeles hotel without turning it into a museum piece. The work had to feel rooted, feminine, artistic, and alive enough to bring a landmark back into conversation.

I helped lead the creative system across the brand book, collateral, launch materials, campaign pieces, and hotel touchpoints: the practical things guests hold, see, keep, and quietly judge.

The result was a visual language built for a hotel with a real past and a very public second act.

Sign your work.

Artists sign the work they are proud of. That was the idea behind the handwritten Hotel Figueroa mark. The hotel was being shaped for the creative class, so the identity needed to feel authored rather than corporate. The signature gave the brand a human hand: personal, expressive, and proud of the experience it was putting into the world.

Not a reset.

The job was not to make Hotel Figueroa feel new. It was to make the history legible again: confident enough for modern downtown Los Angeles, careful enough not to sand down the character that made the place worth bringing back.
